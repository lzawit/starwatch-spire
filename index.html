Starwatch Spire

Creatures of the dark circle the tower until dawn. Eight tolls of the bell stand between you and sunrise.

  1. Hold the phone up like a camera. Turn your body and tilt the phone to look around the battlements.
  2. Tap a spell to cast it at the rune in the middle. Hold Emberlance to keep firing.
  3. Follow the edge arrows to creatures you can't see. Don't let them reach the tower top.

Motion aiming needs a phone and a secure (https) page. Without it, drag the view to look. On a computer: drag to look, keys 1–4 to cast.
